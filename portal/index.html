Concordance

Reading Canada's public record as a hundred-year instrument.

Internet Archive Canada holds 104,241 scanned government publications. Inside them are records of the physical condition of the country — air, water, soil — town by town, from 1841 onward. Concordance tests what becomes possible when those scattered documents are read as one connected historical monitoring record.

Owen Sound, 1963–1972

87 readings recovered from scanned annual reports, 59 of them measurements. Every number links to the page it was read from.

These figures were recovered from scanned paper by a language model, not transcribed by a person. The early four-page benchmark and its failures are published in the repository. Every published number here carries its cited archive page, because a measurement read out of a sixty-year-old scan has no authority on its own — it earns authority by being trivially easy to check.
